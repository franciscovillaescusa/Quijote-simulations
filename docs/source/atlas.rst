.. _atlas:

*****************
Publication Atlas
*****************

Hundreds of papers have been written using the Quijote simulations. The **Quijote Literature Atlas** is an interactive map of all of them, designed to help you explore this body of work, find the papers most relevant to your research, and identify connections between research lines as well as gaps that are still open.

The atlas offers several views, selected with the buttons at the top of the app:

- **Topics**: each dot is a paper, and papers with similar abstracts sit close together, grouped into research themes.
- **Citations**: the citation network between Quijote papers.
- **Co-authors**: the collaboration network between authors.
- **Table**: a searchable list of all papers.
- **Trends**: how the different topics have evolved over time.

You can search for papers or authors, filter by year, and click on any paper to see its details.
